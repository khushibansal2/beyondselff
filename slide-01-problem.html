01 · Problem Statement
Why
BeyondSelf
Exists
Life happens across three domains — health, finance, and career — but people manage each in isolation. No tool connects them. No tool shows you how one affects the other. No tool gives you a single score for how well you're living.
73%
of people feel overwhelmed managing health, money & career simultaneously
🧩
Fragmented Data, Zero Insight
Health apps, finance trackers, and career tools never talk to each other. Users switch between 5+ apps, losing holistic perspective on their own life.
⚡
Hidden Cross-Domain Cascades
Poor sleep leads to emotional spending. Financial stress tanks career readiness. These invisible links are never surfaced — until it's too late.
📉
Reactive, Not Predictive
Existing tools show what happened, not what will happen. No burnout warning. No life score. No simulation of "what if I change this habit?"
🎯
Our Objective
Build an AI-powered Digital Twin that unifies health, finance & career into one intelligent, predictive life score with cross-domain cascade awareness.
